Chocolate Cake Recipe

A guide to making your first chocolate cake

While baking anything seems intimidating, this guide will help you bake your first ever chocolate cake!

Whether it's just a late night treat or a sweet gift to a loved one, the resulting product will be the most delicious thing you've ever put in you mouth!

Note: this recipe yields 12 servings

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 6 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup margarine
  • 1 3/4 cups white sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 cups milk

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degres F (175 degrees C).
  2. Grease and flour a 9x13-inch pan.
  3. Sift together the flour, cocoa baking soda, and salt. Set aside.
  4. In a large bowl, cream together the margarine and sugar until light and fluffy.
  5. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then stir in the vanilla.
  6. Beat in the flour mixture alternately with the milk, mixing just until incorporated.
  7. Pour batter into prepared pan.
  8. Bake in the preheated oven for 40 to 45 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center of the cake comes out clean. Allow to cool.

Congratulations, you've baked your first ever chocolate cake! Have fun eating!
