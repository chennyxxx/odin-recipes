Chocolate Cake Recipe

A guide to making your first chocolate cake

While baking anything seems intimidating, this guide will help you bake your first ever chocolate cake!

Whether it's just a late night treat or a sweet gift to a loved one, the resulting product will be the most delicious thing you've ever put in you mouth!

Note: this recipe yields 12 servings

back to home!

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 6 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup margarine
  • 1 3/4 cups white sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 cups milk
