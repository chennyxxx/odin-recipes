KitKat Fudge Slice Recipe

A guide to a quick and easy KitKat fudge slice

Don't have much time on your plate?"

Don't worry at all! Here's a quick and easy recipe for KitKat fudge slices that only takes three ingredients!

Note: this recipe yields 20 servings

Ingredients

  • 395g Sweetened Condensed Milk
  • 2 x 200g Milk Chocolate Bits
  • 3 x 185g pkts Nestlé funpack KitKat, unwrapped

Steps

  1. Grease and line the base and sides of a 20cm square cake pan with baking paper.
  2. Place the sweetened condensed milk and chocolate bits in a medium-sized saucepan over low heat. Cook, stirring, for 5 to 7 minutes or until melted and combined.
  3. Pour half of the chocolate mixture into the prepared pan. Place a layer of Funpack KitKat chocolate on top. Pour over remaining chocolate mixture and smooth the surface. Arrange another layer of KitKat on top. Cover with plastic wrap and place in the fridge for 2 hours or until set.
  4. Cut into squares to serve.

Congratulations, you've made a quick, easy and absoluetly delectable KitKat Fudge Slice, Enjoy!
